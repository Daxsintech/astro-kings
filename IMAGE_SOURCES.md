# Image sources

Every image and video in `public/`, where it is used, and what is known about it.
**Nothing here is guessed:** a source or licence nobody has confirmed is written as
"unknown" / "unconfirmed". "People recognisable?" comes from looking at the image.
"Notes" come from the files' own metadata or from what is visible in them.

Before launch every row should read **licence confirmed** and, where people are
recognisable, **consent confirmed** (children: written parental consent).
